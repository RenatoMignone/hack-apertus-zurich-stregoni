# Sample documents for upload

Thirteen PDF documents that the four demo customers might upload into their TrustRoom to ask questions about them. All companies, people and numbers are **fictitious**. Each document matches the customer's e-banking data, so a good solution can combine document and transaction data.

All documents are in German, as a Swiss customer would receive them. They are digital PDFs with a text layer. The OCR tool can be used for scans or photos.

---

## Persona 1 – Lukas Meier (`P1_Lukas_Meier/`)

| File | Document | Issuer (fictitious) |
|---|---|---|
| `Lohnabrechnung_2026-11_Lukas_Meier.pdf` | Payslip November 2026 | Limmatblau Versicherungen AG |
| `Vorsorgeausweis_2026_Lukas_Meier.pdf` | Pension fund certificate as of 01.01.2026 | Pensionskasse Limmatblau |

| `Reiseofferte_Japan_2027_Fernweh_Atelier.pdf` | Travel offer Japan, April 2027 | Fernweh Atelier Reisen AG |

Example questions:

- «Does the salary on this payslip match what I received on my account?»
- «Is my partner Sara covered by my pension fund if something happens to me?»

- «Do I get money back for my gym membership?»
- «Can I afford the Japan trip with my current savings, if Sara pays half?»
