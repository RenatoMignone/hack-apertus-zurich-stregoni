## Upload and verify

Sprintd accepts one shared submission per team. Sign in with the account used to apply. The Submission tile or Submit tab opens with the build phase. An assigned teammate can complete the form, attest that the team built the work during the event, and click **Submit project**. The other team members can view or update the shared entry before the cutoff. Check the live form for exact fields. [Sprintd submission guide (sign-in required)](https://app.sprintd.org/events/hack-apertus-zurich/submit/guide) · [Public submission walkthrough](https://hackapertus.notion.site/How-What-to-Submit-044b4fec112a83bf9ee48124896b27c5)

- [ ] Confirm the public repository and video open in a signed-out browser. Record the commit corresponding to the submission.
- [ ] Open both PDFs from the ZIP, check the limits and confirm the report lists each development and runtime model with its version and role.
- [ ] Test the demo from the documented setup. Capture the model call or output that demonstrates Apertus performing the core task. Note what was actually verified about deployment and evaluation.
- [ ] Verify Sprintd shows the entry as submitted after the final edit. Record the submission time and commit in the team handoff.

## Partner pitches and Zurich Finals

Each partner judges its own challenge using criteria in the brief and selects two teams for the finals. The pitching guide gives partner slots of seven minutes plus two minutes of Q&A. The opening deck lists a 5–7 minute pitch, so prepare a five-minute version and confirm the allocated time on-site. The six finalists present for five minutes each with slides and take one jury question. [Pitching guide](https://hackapertus.notion.site/Pitching-how-to-761b4fec112a8285876a0153fd1be21e) · [Opening deck](https://hackapertus.ch/deck-zurich)

| Finals criterion | Evidence to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Purposeful use of AI | The task Apertus performs and a representative output. |
| Technical rigour | A working demo, reproducible checks and measured results. |
| Value, cost and scalability | A concrete partner workflow and the main cost or capacity drivers. |
| Sovereign deployability | The chosen target environment, dependencies and verified deployment steps. |
| Implementation feasibility | What the partner needs to adopt or operate the prototype. |
| Pitch and presentation | Clear problem, demo and conclusion within the time limit. |

[Organizer jury process and criteria](https://hackapertus.notion.site/zurich-main-jury-awards)
